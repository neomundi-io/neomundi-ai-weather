About ControlTower AI

ControlTower AI is the public, extensible brand for real-time observation of AI system behavior. AI Weather™ is its main, current experience.

What ControlTower AI does

ControlTower AI measures the behavioral reliability of AI systems in real time, to help you stay in control and make better decisions. AI Weather™ (the daily observation wall, its widgets, and this site) is its public, daily-updated observatory.

NeoMundi, underneath

The NeoMundi runtime metrology layer produces every measurement and hash-chains each day's result into a timestamped capsule, verifiable independently of ControlTower AI itself.

NeoMundi Observatory → NeoMundi.org ↗

What this is not

This is not a leaderboard. AI Weather™ does not rank AI systems, determine which one is "best", or judge whether individual answers are true or false. Every view shows an observed condition, not a competitive score.
