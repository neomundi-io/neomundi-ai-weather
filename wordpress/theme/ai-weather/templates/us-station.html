FIRST INTERNATIONAL EXPERIMENTAL EXTENSION

NeoMundi Experimental Station US

An experimental, artistic extension of AI Weather™: a collaboration between OmarAGI and NeoMundi. It observes real cities on a live globe, pairing each one's real physical weather with the same day's real, measured AI Weather™ condition, without ever merging the two into a single value.

  • Status: a separate, experimental station. It is not the main AI Weather™ observation wall and does not replace it.
  • Collaboration: OmarAGI × NeoMundi. Individual work credited on the piece itself, below.
  • Physical weather: real, live, from Open-Meteo, per selected city.
  • AI Weather™ signal: the real measured condition for today, from this same repository's weather.json and its verifiable capsule.

Running locally: the "+24h projected" state you may see referenced below is inactive in this build. One of its source files wasn't part of the local capture supplied for this integration. Everything else on this page (the globe, city selection, live physical weather, the real AI Weather™ condition, and the live camera feed) reads real data.

About this station

        
The globe pairs two sources that are never merged: each city's real physical weather (Open-Meteo) and this repository's real, measured AI Weather™ condition for the day (weather.json, verified against its hash-chained capsule). A live public webcam gives each city a real, current view.
