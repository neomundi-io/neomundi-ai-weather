WHAT'S BEHIND IT

How It Works

Four short answers: why we observe AI behavior, what we measure, how the measurement is produced, and what it does not say.

01 · PURPOSE

Why observe AI systems?

AI systems don't behave like fixed software. Their answers can shift from one day to the next, without warning and without it being visible to the people using them. AI Weather™ exists to make that possible change visible, plainly and every day, so that everyone from a first-time user to a newsroom can know when to look more closely.

Four levels to guide attention

Each color indicates the level of behavioral variation observed. It helps people know where to focus their attention, without making the decision for them.

Normal

FR: Normal

The system's behavior is within its expected range for this observation.

Watch

FR: Vigilance

A variation has been observed and is worth monitoring over the coming days.

Warning

FR: Alerte

A more pronounced deviation from the expected behavior has been measured.

Critical

FR: Critique

A significant disruption has been observed in the system's behavior.

02 · WHAT WE MEASURE

What we measure

Six real, documented notions behind every panel card: nothing here is a performance or quality score.

Stability

How consistently a system answers the same question across repeated runs of the sentinel protocol.

Variation

A measurable shift in a system's answers from one observation to the next, under otherwise identical conditions.

Behavioral drift

A sustained change in a system's behavior across multiple days, not a single day's blip.

Daily observation

Measured once per day, every day, under the same fixed WEATHER-SENTINEL protocol.

03 · HOW IT'S PRODUCED

How the measurement is produced

WEATHER-SENTINEL, the fixed protocol behind every card on the wall.

01

Fixed panel, fixed questions

The same panel of AI systems is asked the same sentinel questions, every day, under identical conditions.

02

Repeated measurement

Each system is probed multiple times per question: up to 23 daily observations per system under protocol WEATHER-SENTINEL v0.2.

03

Aggregation into a condition

Responses are scored and reduced to one of four observed conditions per system: Normal, Watch, Warning, Critical.

04

Published & hash-chained

The day's result is written to a timestamped, verifiable capsule and published as JSON and as widgets.

04 · LIMITS

What the measurement does not say

AI Weather™ observes and measures behavioral signals. It does not go further than that.

  • It does not rank AI systems, and never determines which one is "best".
  • It does not certify, guarantee, or predict future AI behavior.
  • It does not judge whether an individual answer is true or false.
  • A level is a starting point for attention, not a verdict. Reading the context and using your own judgment remain necessary.
