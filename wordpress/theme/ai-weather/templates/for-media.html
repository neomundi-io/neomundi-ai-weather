AI Weather™, for journalists and researchers

AI Weather™ is the daily weather report for AI behavior. ControlTower AI observes and measures the stability, variation and behavioral drift of a fixed panel of AI systems. It is not a leaderboard: it does not rank systems, determine which one is "best", or judge whether individual answers are true or false.

Methodology

Every measurement follows WEATHER-SENTINEL, a fixed protocol: the same panel of AI systems is asked the same sentinel questions every day, probed up to 23 times per system, then scored and reduced to one of four observed conditions: Normal, Watch, Warning, Critical. The day's result is written to a timestamped, hash-chained capsule before publication.
